A.7.20 â€?Consolidated cross-platform statements

A.7.20.1 Open-screen / splash ads

Triggered on cold-launch and resume-from-background. SDKs may use cached ad payloads to reduce perceived latency. Some SDKs transmit a wake event with timestamp, app version, and locale.

A.7.20.2 Rewarded video ads

User-initiated. The user taps a "watch" affordance, the SDK plays a full-screen video, and on SDK-verified completion the app grants an in-app reward. Failure modes (no fill, network error) are surfaced in-app without awarding the reward.

A.7.20.3 Interstitial ads

Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points (between screens, after key tasks, on a session boundary). Frequency caps are configured per session at the app layer; SDK-side caps also apply.

A.7.20.4 Banner ads

Persistent strip at the top or bottom of the screen. SDKs refresh at platform-defined intervals (commonly 30â€?20 s). Refresh rates are configurable per SDK.

A.7.20.5 Frequency capping and mediation

Frequency capping and mediation waterfall are implemented at the app layer using capped partners and timeouts. We periodically review partner performance and rotate to optimise fill and latency.

A.7.20.6 Children's ad treatment

Personalised advertising is forbidden for users known to be under the relevant age threshold (see Â§A.8). Contextual ads may still be used; behavioural profiling (already removed in Â§A.8) is disabled.
